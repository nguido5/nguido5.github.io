Protocol-Injected Driver & Gated Canary Safety Architecture

  • Structural Protocol DI: Abstracted hardware behind a runtime-checkable Controller protocol (protocol.py), separating pure ASCII command builders/parsers (commands.py: !G, ?HS, ?C, ?A, ?BA, ?V 2, ?FS, !MSS) from RoboteqController async serial I/O so unit tests run without hardware.
  • Startup Resync & Layered Watchdog: On connect (10s timeout), driver halts telemetry streaming, drains stale bytes (1s hard cap), disables/saves echo to EEPROM, serializes transactions via asyncio.Lock, and refreshes nonzero commands every 0.5 s against the hardware’s ~1.0 s watchdog alongside finally stop blocks.
  • Canary Gating: Sequential orchestrator gates high-energy tests: failing initial Hall connectivity skips all motor commands; failing hall_tune or the 1.5s open-loop spin canary auto-skips downstream motion tests.

Sensor Commissioning & Closed-Loop RPM Acceptance Math

  • MSS Hall Angle Calibration (run_hall_tune): Triggers !MSS and polls status bit 0x100 (0.5s interval, 90s timeout) while masking expected pre-cal sensor fault bit 0x20. Verifies shaft sweep ≥ 500 encoder counts, validates the 12-entry Hall Sensor Angle Table (HSAT, 0–511 electrical angle scale ≠ 128 default) and winding-swap flag before EEPROM save.
  • Combined Hall & Monotonic Encoder Validation: Samples both sensors in one 3.0s spin (50 ms poll): requires all valid Hall states [1..6] with zero forbidden states [0, 7], total encoder Δ ≥ 10,000 counts, and strict sign monotonicity across noise-filtered deltas (|Δi| > 1).
  • RPM & Current Cutoffs: Scales command u = round((RPM/4250)×1000) (u=706 at 3000 RPM). Enforces ±1.1865% steady-state mean RPM window, ≤ 6 A mean motor current at 3000 RPM, and instantaneous cutoffs at 15 A (3000 RPM) and 40 A (4250 RPM).

Bayesian 64-State POMDP Estimator (BeliefTracker)

  • Transition Sanitization & Burn-In: Sanitizes incoming 64×64 transition matrices (perturbed by εij ∼ U(−0.1, 0.1) in the game engine) against NaN/Inf and zero-sum rows, caches matrix powers (T, T²), and computes the exact 1,000-step rat head-start distribution (b1000 = b0·T¹⁰⁰⁰) from uniform prior b0(i) = 1/64.
  • Multi-Modal Bayesian Sensor Fusion: Extracts 64-bit board masks (_space, _primed, _carpet, _blocked) to evaluate floor-dependent acoustic likelihoods P(noise | x) and noisy Manhattan distance likelihoods P(d | x) with offset distribution e ∈ {−1, 0, +1, +2} at P(e) = (0.12, 0.70, 0.12, 0.06), clamping negative estimates at d = 0: bt+1(x) ∝ [bt(x)·T^k] · P(noise | cell(x)) · P(d_obs | Manhattan(o, x))
  • Search Feedback & Pre-Warmed JAX Rollouts: Zeros out missed search cells, resets to steady-state b1000 on captures, and pre-warms @jax.jit matrix-vector kernels during __init__ so multi-ply forward belief rollouts (predict_layers) incur zero JIT compilation latency during timed play.

Horizon Planning, Macro Context & Sensing Arbitration (SearchEngine)

  • Iterative-Deepening Negamax & Move Ordering: Explores immutable forecast_move states up to depth 13 using alpha-beta pruning, a 120,000-entry Transposition Table (EXACT, LOWER, UPPER bounds keyed on bitmasks, positions, scores, and turns), 3-slot killer moves, and history heuristics.
  • Belief-Conditioned Evaluation & Macro Horizon: Scores leaves via V = 115·P_Δ + 46·Q_carpet + 17.5·C_conv + 4.4·M_mob + 16·R_Δ + 1.1·C_center, weighting top-6 posterior rat cells by inverse Manhattan distance. MacroContext evaluates 2–4 turn horizons to build high-scoring length 4–7 carpet runs (+6 to +21 pts) while penalizing premature short rolls.
  • Opponent Tempo & Sense-vs-Act Arbitration: OpponentTempoTracker diffs 64-bit masks to infer opponent move types and boost safe PRIME setups during low-impact opponent turns. Decouples SEARCH from tree branching, substituting a rat search only when calibrated expected value EV = 4(0.9p) - 2(1 - 0.9p) beats board gain across phase, lead/deficit, cooldown, and opponent hit-rate gates.

Implemented Physics Engine & Numerical Solvers (Airplane.cpp)

  • Polymorphic Architecture: Abstract Airplane base class encapsulates geometry (S, AR, W), parabolic drag polar (CD = CD0 + k·CL²), two-layer ISA atmosphere, and maneuver solvers, while concrete subclasses override pure virtual thrustAvailable(V, h) and powerAvailable(V, h):
    • Propeller: Pshaft = max(0, PSL·(1.132σ − 0.132)), TA = ηp·Pshaft / V.
    • Turbojet: TA = max(0, TSL·σ^m), PA = TA·V.
    • Turbofan: First-order Mach lapse TA = max(0, TSL·σ^m·(1 − c·M)).
  • Two-Region ISA Atmosphere (Airplane::isa): Models tropospheric lapse (h ≤ 11 km, T = T0 − L·h, P = P0·(T/T0)^(g/RL)) and isothermal stratosphere (h > 11 km, exponential pressure decay) to return AtmosphereState {T, P, rho, a}.
  • Numerical Optimization & Integration Methods:
    • bestClimbSpeed(h): 100-iteration Golden-Section Search (ε = 10⁻³ m/s) over [max(1.0, 1.001·Vstall), 300 m/s] maximizing specific excess power Ps = (PA − PR)/W.
    • serviceCeiling(): Dynamic bracket expansion (20 km → 50 km cap) followed by 70-iteration Bisection root-finder for ROCmax(h) = 0.508 m/s (100 ft/min).
    • timeToClimb(h1, h2): 1,000-interval Composite Simpson’s Rule (1, 4, 2, ..., 4, 1) integrating ∫ dh / ROC(h) with singularity guards (ROC < 10⁻⁹ m/s → ∞).
  • Sustained Turn & Pull-Up Envelope: Computes stall limit nstall = q·S·CLmax / W and Ps = 0 thrust limit nthrust = √((TA − q·S·CD0)·q·S / (k·W²)) to evaluate turn rate ψ̇ = g√(n² − 1)/V, turn radius R = V²/(g√(n² − 1)), and pull-up radius R = V²/(g(n − 1)).

Next-Phase Architecture Expansion: Wiring in dormant TrajSim dependencies (Matrix.h, Vector_JD.h, EquilFlow.h) and refactoring from inheritance to decoupled subsystem composition with numerical ODE trajectory integration and Euler/quaternion attitude kinematics.
