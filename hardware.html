4. Custom FPV Quadcopter Build, Instrumentation & Betaflight 4.4.3 PID Flight Testing

STM32F405 • BMI270 Gyro • 50A 4-in-1 ESC • Blackbox Logging • PID Tuning

Hardware Trade Study, Stack Architecture & Flight Test Methodology

  • Confirmed Airframe & Avionics Stack: Executed cost–performance trade studies to select, assemble, and solder a 5-inch freestyle testbed: STEELE 5 (220 mm, 5 mm carbon arm) frame, SpeedyBee F405 V3 stack (STM32F405 MCU, BMI270 gyro, onboard barometer, microSD Blackbox, 50A 4-in-1 ESC), Racerstar BR2205 2300KV brushless motors, HQProp 5×4.3×3 V2S tri-blade polycarbonate props, 14.8V 4S 1300mAh 100C LiPo, Foxeer Razer Mini 1200TVL camera, EWRF 5.8GHz 650mW VTX, and Hall-gimbal JumperRC T-Lite V2 OpenTX transmitter.
  • Decoupled Propulsion vs. Controls Testing: Separated static/climb propulsion efficiency from closed-loop PID attitude tuning. Quantified electrical input power (P = V·I) via inline wattmeter and vertical climb kinematics (a ≈ 2h/t²) across fixed altitude intervals to evaluate thrust-to-power efficiency (ηT = T/P, achieving a +15% improvement).
  • Controlled 5-Stage Betaflight 4.4.3 PID Tuning: Backed up CLI config and flashed target SPEEDYBEEF405V3 with microSD Blackbox logging. Executed sequential single-parameter flight test sweeps: (1) Baseline oscillation/overshoot characterization → (2) P-gain attitude error responsiveness → (3) D-gain prop-wash damping vs. motor thermal noise → (4) I-gain sustained attitude hold & disturbance rejection → (5) Repeatability validation.

5. "First Step" Lunar EVA Boot Multi-Sensor Fusion Platform — 1st Place, GT AIAA Hackathon

UWB GPR (1.5–3 GHz) • MEMS FT-NIR • PZT Energy Harvesting • SM-24 Geophone

Designed a 3-inch Kevlar/rubber sensorized EVA boot sole and rigid rear-ankle electronics housing that collects, filters, normalizes, and cross-references 4 sensor modalities on every astronaut footstep against the NASA Planetary Data System (PDS) library to flag subsurface voids and classify ISRU minerals in real time:
