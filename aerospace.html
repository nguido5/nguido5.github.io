Implemented Physics Engine & Numerical Solvers (Airplane.cpp)

  • Polymorphic Architecture: Abstract Airplane base class encapsulates geometry (S, AR, W), parabolic drag polar (CD = CD0 + k·CL²), two-layer ISA atmosphere, and maneuver solvers, while concrete subclasses override pure virtual thrustAvailable(V, h) and powerAvailable(V, h):
    • Propeller: Pshaft = max(0, PSL·(1.132σ − 0.132)), TA = ηp·Pshaft / V.
    • Turbojet: TA = max(0, TSL·σ^m), PA = TA·V.
    • Turbofan: First-order Mach lapse TA = max(0, TSL·σ^m·(1 − c·M)).
  • Two-Region ISA Atmosphere (Airplane::isa): Models tropospheric lapse (h ≤ 11 km, T = T0 − L·h, P = P0·(T/T0)^(g/RL)) and isothermal stratosphere (h > 11 km, exponential pressure decay) to return AtmosphereState {T, P, rho, a}.
  • Numerical Optimization & Integration Methods:
    • bestClimbSpeed(h): 100-iteration Golden-Section Search (ε = 10⁻³ m/s) over [max(1.0, 1.001·Vstall), 300 m/s] maximizing specific excess power Ps = (PA − PR)/W.
    • serviceCeiling(): Dynamic bracket expansion (20 km → 50 km cap) followed by 70-iteration Bisection root-finder for ROCmax(h) = 0.508 m/s (100 ft/min).
    • timeToClimb(h1, h2): 1,000-interval Composite Simpson’s Rule (1, 4, 2, ..., 4, 1) integrating ∫ dh / ROC(h) with singularity guards (ROC < 10⁻⁹ m/s → ∞).
  • Sustained Turn & Pull-Up Envelope: Computes stall limit nstall = q·S·CLmax / W and Ps = 0 thrust limit nthrust = √((TA − q·S·CD0)·q·S / (k·W²)) to evaluate turn rate ψ̇ = g√(n² − 1)/V, turn radius R = V²/(g√(n² − 1)), and pull-up radius R = V²/(g(n − 1)).

Next-Phase Architecture Expansion: Wiring in dormant TrajSim dependencies (Matrix.h, Vector_JD.h, EquilFlow.h) and refactoring from inheritance to decoupled subsystem composition with numerical ODE trajectory integration and Euler/quaternion attitude kinematics.

Multi-Element NACA 4412 Airfoil RANS CFD Analysis (ANSYS Fluent, Re ≈ 200,000, V∞ = 30 m/s)

ANSYS Fluent 2026 R1 • k-ω SST Turbulence • Structured C-Mesh • Slat/Flap Trade Study

Conducted 2D planar steady-state pressure-based RANS simulations using a structured C-mesh and k-ω SST turbulence model to resolve adverse pressure gradients and boundary-layer separation across a clean NACA 4412 baseline and three high-lift configurations (α = 0°). Personally executed the ANSYS Fluent simulations for Configuration A (5° Slat / 13° Flap) and Configuration B (20° Slat / 13° Flap):
